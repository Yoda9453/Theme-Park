Theme Park

Trend following across thematic equities.

As of 2026-10-13

Theme Park is a long-only trend book of thematic equities. A name is held only while a channel signal is on and price remains above its stop. Risk is shared across industry groups by the square root of names in trend, then sized to a portfolio volatility target.
